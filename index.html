Personality Assessment

140 public-domain items. Scored against empirical norms from 410,376 people. Blunt, not flattering.

What this actually is

Two instruments, both public domain, both with published validity evidence:

IPIP-NEO-120Johnson (2014) 120 items measuring the Big Five across 30 facets. Facet reliabilities .64–.88.
IPIP HEXACO-HAshton & Lee 20 items measuring Honesty-Humility — the trait the Big Five omits.

Your percentiles come from Johnson's validation dataset, restricted to your own sex and age band, using exact tie-corrected ranks — not a bell-curve approximation. No norms are invented.

Privacy

Scoring happens entirely in this page. Your answers never leave your device, and there is no server, no account and no analytics. Progress is saved to this browser's local storage so you can stop and resume; “Delete my data” erases it.

This gives blunt output. It reports what the numbers say, including the unflattering parts. It is a research-grade self-report questionnaire, not a clinical assessment, and it cannot diagnose anything.

Norm group

Personality norms differ by sex and age. These two answers select your comparison group; they are not scored.
