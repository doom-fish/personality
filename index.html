Psychometrics

Public-domain tests, scored against percentile norms computed from real open datasets. Blunt, not flattering. Nothing is uploaded.

How this differs from the usual online test

Every percentile here is an exact tie-corrected rank against a named dataset of real respondents of your own sex and age band — not a bell-curve approximation and not an invented norm. Every scoring key was verified by reproducing the instrument's published reliabilities before any score was interpreted. Where honest norms do not exist, no percentile is shown.

Privacy

Scoring happens entirely in this page. Your answers never leave your device, and there is no server, no account and no analytics. Progress is saved to this browser's local storage so you can stop and resume; “Delete my data” erases it.

Norm group

Informant ratings are the cheapest way around the main weakness of self-report. Ask someone who knows you well to take it about you, export both results and compare them — observers see traits like Extraversion and Conscientiousness more accurately than you do, and private ones like Neuroticism less accurately.

Before you start. This questionnaire asks about low mood, anxiety and stress in the past week. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and this page gives no medical advice. If you are struggling, findahelpline.com lists free helplines by country.
